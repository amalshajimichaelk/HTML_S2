Welcome to St. Antony's Higher Secondary School, Poonjar! We are dedicated to providing quality education and holistic development to our students. Our school is a nurturing environment that encourages academic excellence and personal growth.

About Us

St. Antony's Higher Secondary School, located in Poonjar, has been a beacon of educational excellence for many years. Founded in 1950, we have consistently focused on nurturing young minds and preparing them for success in their future endeavors.

Our school offers a well-rounded curriculum that focuses on academics, sports, arts, and character development. We believe in providing a balanced education that equips our students with the skills and values needed for a successful life.

Our Courses

We offer a variety of subjects for students at the higher secondary level. Below are some of the key courses available:

  • Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)
  • Mathematics
  • Commerce (Accountancy, Business Studies, Economics)
  • Humanities (History, Political Science, Geography)
  • Physical Education
